## Conversion principles for this audience
- Malaysian families, majority Melayu, BM language
- Biggest hook: **RM20 sebulan** (harga) + **jenama Coway** (trust)
- They fear: "mahal", "susah daftar", "tipu", "tak tau mana nak mula"
- Answer those fears directly
- Short sentences. Scannable. Clear benefit.
- Setiap ayat kena ada reason to act
